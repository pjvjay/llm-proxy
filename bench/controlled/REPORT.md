## Sweep A — how much of System 1's win is structure vs assumed model speed (W1)

The single-pass structure cuts forward passes regardless of model speed — by half for roles that need two labels (user messages), not at all for single-label roles (responses), so −35% on this mix. The *latency* win over tier-0 depends on the System-1 model's per-window cost, which is an assumption; with a slow System-1 model, route single-label roles to their dedicated head (`hybrid`).

`hybrid` = multi-label model for user messages and tool results, the dedicated 33 ms toxicity head for responses (`DECISION_SINGLE_LABEL_ROLES=assistant`).
